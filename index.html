TOUCH

Auto-thrust. Drag the left stick to steer; push up for nose down, pull down for nose up. Airbrake into sharp turns. Fire with the right thumb. At the start, hold BRAKE to lower thrust; release to build it. Aim for the marked 65–80% band at GO. Landscape gives the best view; VIEW switches cameras.

THE CIRCUIT

Cyan BOOST pads accelerate. Amber/magenta WEAPON pads arm one weapon. Follow FORK signs and pick a lane before the split. Keep your speed over the jump. Wall contact scrubs velocity.

LAUNCH

Build thrust with W / ↑ or A / R2 and reach 65–80% at GO for a launch boost. Touch pilots must hold and release BRAKE to time the launch; automatic thrust alone does not earn it.

Five laps. Eight pilots. No rubber-banding. Time attack disables weapons. Records stay in this browser.
